    Quiz Triggers

    Run actions automatically when someone completes a quiz — tag them in your email service provider, send a webhook, start an Ultimatum countdown, or grant Apprentice access.

    ⓘ

    Quiz Triggers fire once when someone completes a quiz, based on their overall result. If you want to tag people based on individual answers they pick, use Tagged Answers instead.
